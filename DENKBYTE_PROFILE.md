# DenkByte - Company Profile

## 🏢 Company Overview

**DenkByte** is a technology solutions company specializing in intelligent systems,
data analytics, and digital transformation. We combine creative thinking ("Denk")
with cutting-edge technology ("Byte") to deliver innovative solutions that drive
real-world impact.

**Location**: Chemnitz, Germany
**Context**: Fictional company created for the Planspiel course at TU Chemnitz (WS 2026/27)

## 🎯 Core Services

### 1. AI & Automation Consulting 🤖
Strategic guidance on AI implementation, automation opportunities, and digital transformation.

### 2. Custom Software Development 💻
Bespoke, cloud-native solutions tailored to business needs — scalable, secure, and maintainable.

### 3. Data Analytics & BI 📊
Transforming raw data into actionable insights with analytics, predictive modeling, and dashboards.

### 4. Cloud Infrastructure ☁️
Secure, scalable cloud solutions — architecture design, migration, optimization, and managed services.

### 5. Performance Optimization ⚡
Enhancing system efficiency and reducing operational costs through tuning and architecture improvements.

### 6. Security & Compliance 🔒
Robust security frameworks ensuring data protection and regulatory compliance (GDPR, ISO 27001).
